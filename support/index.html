Planetshine — Support

Planetshine (星球放晴) is a cozy hex-nonogram planet game, made by one person. If something is wrong, confusing, or you have an idea, write to hello.planetshine@gmail.com; a real person reads it.

Lost progress

Your progress lives on your device. Before changing phones, open Me → Backup code and copy it; on the new phone, paste it in the same place. The Explorer's Pass can always be restored from Me → Restore purchases, signed in to the same store account.

The Explorer's Pass did not unlock

Open Me → Restore purchases. If the store says the purchase went through and the Pass is still not active after a restart, write to us with the date and the store (App Store or Google Play).

Languages

The game follows your phone's language. To change it: tap the language name on the welcome screen, or Me → Settings → the first row.
